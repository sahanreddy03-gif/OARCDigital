## ANALYTICS — Restaurant Business Data

**URL (proposed):** `/h360/restaurant-analytics-without-pos-malta`

**Plain-language (the 5-year-old line — use as the human hook):** It tells you how your restaurant is doing. How many customers came. How much money you made. What people liked.


**GATE 4 — the literal search the owner types:** `restaurant analytics without pos malta`
**GATE 2 — the exact question to answer for the AI (verbatim, self-contained answer required on page):** "how do I track my restaurant numbers without a POS"


**THE WEDGE (Gate 3 — why ONLY we can say this):**
**Every analytics product needs data from somewhere — POS, online ordering, reservations.**

Most small restaurants in Malta have NONE of these. They have a cash register and a notebook.

**ANALYTICS builds data from one simple input: the daily total.**

The owner types in today's total revenue at closing. That's it. ANALYTICS does the rest.

**"I made €1,200 today." — that's all the system needs.**

---

**EXPERT-FAILS LOGIC (master the experts, then beat them — keep this on the page as the contrast):**
- **7Shifts (Staff scheduling + analytics, $40/mo)** fails the small Malta restaurant: Focused on LABOR analytics, not full business analytics. You need a POS integration for sales data. Small restaurants don't have POS data to connect.
- **Toast Analytics (Included with Toast POS)** fails the small Malta restaurant: You need Toast POS. Toast POS is €50-€200/month per terminal. Small restaurants don't use Toast in Malta.
- **Olo GDP (Enterprise, $500+/mo)** fails the small Malta restaurant: $500+/mo. Requires multiple data sources. Designed for chains with digital ordering.
- **Excel / Google Sheets (Free)** fails the small Malta restaurant: Nobody updates it consistently. The data is only as good as the last entry. No alerts. No trends. No insights — just raw numbers that need human interpretation.
- **Cash Register Memory (Free)** fails the small Malta restaurant: Memory is unreliable. "It felt busier" is not data. You can't track trends, spot problems, or make informed decisions. A restaurant that operates on gut feel is a restaurant that leaks money.

**FAILURE MODES (already solved — these become the page's FAQ/objection-handling, do NOT drop):**
- *Objection:* "The owner doesn't enter the daily number" → *Answer:* WhatsApp reminder at 9pm: "How much did you make today? Reply with the number." If they don't reply by 10pm, another reminder. If they miss 3 days, we ask: "Everything ok?"
- *Objection:* "The average spend per customer is just a guess" → *Answer:* We start with a guess (€22 for a normal restaurant). After 30 days, we calculate the actual average from the revenue/customer relationship. It gets more accurate over time.
- *Objection:* "One high-revenue day skews the data" → *Answer:* We use rolling 7-day and 30-day averages. One spike or dip doesn't distort the trend.
- *Objection:* "The restaurant has seasonal swings" → *Answer:* Summer tourism creates huge swings. ANALYTICS compares this week to the same week last year, not last month. After a year, seasonal patterns become clear.
- *Objection:* "Cash tips are not tracked" → *Answer:* Cash tips are income the owner keeps separately. ANALYTICS tracks RESTAURANT revenue, not the owner's wallet. If tips matter, the owner adds them to the daily number.
- *Objection:* "The restaurant has multiple tills" → *Answer:* Owner adds the totals from all tills before entering: "Till 1: €800 + Till 2: €400 = €1,200." One number.
- *Objection:* "The owner already uses an accounting tool" → *Answer:* ANALYTICS is different. Accounting tools track PREVIOUS month for tax purposes. ANALYTICS tracks TODAY for operational decisions. Both can coexist.
