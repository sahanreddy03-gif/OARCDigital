A/B tests:

Video hero vs static hero image

Multi-step application form vs single-step lead form

Progressive profiling:

First convert: name + email + budget band; follow-up form: company + website

6 — Grand Slam Offer integration (how it becomes the goldmine)

The Grand Slam Offer is the lead magnet: 12–16 page PDF containing your “Grand Slam materials” — value stacks, 2–3 mini case studies, creative examples, typical results, and a one-page audit checklist.

Place prominent CTA for “Download Grand Slam Offer” in hero and in Core Offer section.

Gated download qualifies the lead (ask budget band).

After download: Immediate email + brief value video and link to calendly.

Use the PDF as a retargeting asset: people who opened but did not book get a 3-email nudge and remarketing ads.

Why it works:

Shows you did the homework

Lets you send the heavy bonus content without posting it publicly

Filters and qualifies leads by budget band on download
