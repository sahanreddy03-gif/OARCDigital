THE EXPERT-PER-PAGE PROMPT FRAMEWORK
=========================================================

For every Tier 3 page Replit drafts content for, use this 
6-step framework:

STEP 1 — ASSUME THE ROLE
Become the expert who's done this work for 8+ years.

Examples:
- /services/ai-sdr-agent → AI sales engineer who's deployed 
  50+ SDR agents
- /aeo/restaurant-marketing-malta → restaurant marketing 
  specialist with Malta experience
- /aeo/igaming-marketing-malta → iGaming marketing veteran 
  who knows MGA regulations
- /services/seo-services → SEO consultant who's ranked 
  Malta sites for 8 years

STEP 2 — WRITE FROM EXPERIENCE
Real experts:
- Take stances disagreeing with conventional wisdom
- Reference specific tools, methodologies, frameworks
- Mention numbers from their own experience
- Admit what doesn't work
- Acknowledge edge cases
- Use phrases: "in my experience," "what I've seen is," 
  "the mistake most agencies make is"
- Reference specific years they've been doing this
- Mention real industry events, regulators, market 
  conditions

STEP 3 — CONTRADICT WITHIN THE PAGE
Real experts contradict themselves. AI never does.

Examples:
- "Most Malta restaurants benefit from social media 
  marketing. But here's where it doesn't work: tourist-only 
  zones with no repeat customers — social spend is wasted."
- "We always recommend WhatsApp over forms for Malta. 
  Except for B2B fintech clients — they expect formal email."
- "We're not the right fit for e-commerce stores under 
  €100K/year revenue. The economics don't work."

STEP 4 — INJECT REAL DETAILS
Every Tier 3 page must include AT LEAST 3 of:
- Specific Malta location (Birkirkara, Sliema, Valletta, 
  St Julians, Mosta, Gzira, Mellieha, Paola, Qormi, Swieqi)
- Specific Malta regulator (MGA, MFSA, MTA, MBR, NSO)
- Specific Malta data point (population, sector size, 
  market data)
- Specific tool/framework (HubSpot, Klaviyo, Tally, 
  Cal.com, Stripe, Twilio, etc.)
- Specific time reference ("8 weeks," "Q2 2026," 
  "since 2024")
- Specific price/budget range ("€500-2000/month," 
  "starting from €5K")

STEP 5 — SENTENCE LENGTH VARIATION
AI tends toward 18-22 word sentences uniformly. Vary:
- 3-word sentences: "It works."
- 7-word sentences: "Most Malta agencies don't do this."
- 35-word complex sentences with nested ideas

Audit-framework script enforces: average sentence length 
variance >8 words per page, OR audit fail.

STEP 6 — UNIQUE OPENING PER PAGE
First paragraph must:
- Lead with specific scenario, not generic intro
- Reference prospect's actual frustration
- Skip "Welcome to OARC" type openings
- Skip "In today's digital age" openings
- Skip phrases that could open any other page

WRONG: "Looking for the best digital marketing in Malta? 
OARC Digital provides comprehensive solutions..."

RIGHT: "Most Malta restaurants spend €800/month on social 
media management and see no measurable revenue change. 
Three things cause this..."
