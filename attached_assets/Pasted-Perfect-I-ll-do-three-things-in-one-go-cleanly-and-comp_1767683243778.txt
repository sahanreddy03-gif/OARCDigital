## ROLE OF HIGH-QUALITY CAROUSELS

Carousels serve **three distinct purposes** in your system:

### A. EDUCATION (TOP OF FUNNEL)

Used to:

* Explain problems
* Reframe thinking
* Show why creative fails

Example:

* “Why most creative doesn’t convert”
* “Pretty posts vs revenue-driven creative”

These build **authority and trust**.

---

### B. PROOF & DEPTH (MID FUNNEL)

Used to:

* Show real work
* Show structure behind creativity
* Show platform mastery

This is where your **high-quality carousel designs shine**.

These should:

* Look premium
* Move slowly (no aggressive animations)
* Use confident spacing and typography

---

### C. DECISION SUPPORT (BOTTOM FUNNEL)

Used to:

* Summarise packages
* Highlight bonuses
* Reinforce guarantees

These do **not** sell aggressively — they confirm decisions.
