What Good Social Media Looks Like in Malta

The best social media content in Malta is specific. Not "a great restaurant in Malta" but "the best Southern fried chicken in Qormi." Not "a pizza place in Sliema" but "the Neapolitan pizza that takes 72 hours to make, right here on Triq Manwel Dimech." Specificity wins because Malta is small enough that specific references feel personal — and personal content performs.

We rotate three content styles for every brand: ingredient storytelling (the product is the hero), bold brand confidence (provocative, opinion-driven), and Malta cultural references (local, insider, specific). Never the same style twice in a row. Variety is what keeps audiences coming back.

Optimal posting times for Malta audiences: 12:00–13:00 (lunch scroll), 18:00–20:00 (evening scroll), 21:00–22:00 (night scroll). We never post before 10am or after 11pm — Malta audiences are not active and algorithms do not reward posts that fail to engage in the first hour.
