## SECTION 1 — THE UNIFIED DOCTRINE: THREE HEROES × FOUR GATES (THE BRAIN)

**This is the USP. Print it. Tape it above the monitor. It governs every single word on every single page.**

There are two scales of the same weapon. Fuse them or both fail:

- **At the product/page scale — the THREE HEROES — chosen *in order*:** every page asks what MORE this product becomes for **Hero 1 (the business owner — our customer): how does it make him more money or save him money?** Then **Hero 2 (the owner's guest — our customer's customer): what do they gain so the experience is better and they return to him?** Only AFTER those two are answered does **Hero 3 (OARC) appear — and Hero 3's job is keywords / SEO / AEO / AI-citation / distribution.** We win by making the first two win first. Skip Hero 1 or 2 and the page collapses into "same but better, it integrates, bla bla" — the forbidden mistake. The Three Heroes is *what* the page is about and *for whom*, in strict order.

- **At the word scale — the FOUR GATES — applied to every element:** once the Three Heroes have decided the substance, every headline, paragraph, button label, FAQ, alt-text, meta, and schema property is forced through four masters simultaneously: ranked by Google, citable by AI, true to how the owner thinks, true to how he searches — then Devil's-Advocate'd, then polished. The Four Gates are *how* the substance gets expressed in language.

**The unified law, one sentence:** *Decide what to say by serving the three heroes in order (1 then 2 then 3); decide how to say it by forcing every word through the four gates.* If a page is missing Hero 1 or 2, it's not yet a product — it's a feature list. If a word fails a gate, it isn't finished. Both must be true before anything ships.

Before any element ships, it must clear **both** scales. If it fails one, it is not finished. This is the rule that turns "writing a website" into "manufacturing 21+ products, each a single page, each one obsessively researched and written like a launch."

### GATE 1 — Does Google rank it?  *(Hero 3's job — built on top of Hero 1 + 2 substance, never as a substitute for it)*
- Contains the real keyword a Maltese owner types, in natural language, in the **first 100 words** and the H1 or H2.
- Has correct heading hierarchy (one H1, logical H2/H3).
- Has the right **schema** for its page type (see Section 4).
- Is genuinely useful and original — not thin, not templated-with-keyword-swapped. *Usefulness comes from Hero 1 + Hero 2 actually being served on the page; if they're not, no keyword density saves it.* This is the doorway-page trap that the existing /aeo/ pages fell into. Depth is non-negotiable.
- Internally links to its sibling pages in the cluster.

### GATE 2 — Does AI cite it? (AEO — the uncontested layer, our biggest edge)
- States facts as **clear entities and relationships** an LLM can lift cleanly. ("OARC operates restaurants in Malta and runs their marketing" — not "we're passionate about hospitality solutions.")
- Has at least one **question-and-answer block** phrased exactly as a person would ask an AI ("Why is my restaurant not showing on Google Maps in Malta?").
- Self-contained: the answer to the page's core question is fully on the page, not "contact us to find out."
- Is referenced in `/llms.txt` (see Section 4).
- Remember: **AI gives ONE answer, not a ranked list of ten.** We are not trying to be #3. We are trying to be the single source the AI pulls from. That only happens through topical authority — narrow and deep beats broad and shallow.

### GATE 3 — Does it match how the owner THINKS? *(Hero 1 + Hero 2 live here — this is the gate where "what MORE the product becomes" gets written)*
- **Hero 1 (the owner) must be on the page:** for this specific product, what MORE does it let him make or save? Don't list what it does; show the new money or saved money it unlocks for him. If a competitor's page only says what their product does, ours says what *ours makes happen for him*. (E.g. for the QR menu: not "shows your menu" but "every scan becomes data he never had — what's viewed but not ordered, what to upsell at hunger-peak, which dish to kill — his cheapest market-research and upsell engine, with no POS.")
- **Hero 2 (the guest) must be on the page:** what does the guest GAIN so the experience is better and they return to Hero 1? Show this explicitly — not as a feel-good line, as a mechanism. (E.g. for the QR menu: "the menu remembers them, photos + allergen flags + most-ordered-by-visitors so a tourist orders confidently instead of anxiously — they feel known, they come back to him.") Hero 2 winning *is* Hero 1's repeat business.
- Uses **his words for his pain**, not industry jargon. He doesn't think "omnichannel guest engagement." He thinks *"why is my place empty on a Tuesday."*
- Names the fear underneath: losing the restaurant, bleeding 25–30% to Wolt/Bolt, watching a worse restaurant across the road be full.
- Speaks peer-to-peer (operator to operator), never agency-down-to-client.
- The underlying driver is always **fear of losing** + **desire to be the full one.** Every emotional beat ladders to that.

### GATE 4 — Does it match how the owner SEARCHES?
- The page title and H1 mirror the **literal query**: "why aren't people coming to my restaurant," "how to get more customers restaurant Malta," "restaurant not showing on Google Maps," "get more Google reviews restaurant."
- Long-tail and local: always anchor to **Malta** and, where relevant, **specific localities** (Sliema, St Julian's, Valletta, Paceville, Qormi, etc.).
- One page = one primary query intent. Don't make a page chase five queries; make five pages.

### THEN — Devil's Advocate + Polish (the final two passes)
- **Devil's Advocate pass — hunt the forbidden mistake first:** the page is dead if it reads as "we do the same as them, but better, and it integrates, bla bla." That is the universal slop a generic competitor writes. Read every section asking: *did I just list what the product DOES, or did I show what MORE it BECOMES for Hero 1 and Hero 2?* If it's the first, rewrite until it's the second. Then: where is it generic? Where could *any* agency have written this? Where's the unprovable claim? Where's the jargon? If a sentence survives "a Warsaw agency with a stock photo could have written this exact line" — rewrite it until only *we* could have written it.
- **Polish pass:** punchy, rhythmic, confident. Short sentences for impact. Read it aloud. If it's boring, it failed.

**OpenClaw's job is to audit BOTH scales on every page and produce a structured pass/fail:**
1. **Hero check** (page-scale): is Hero 1's "what MORE he makes/saves" present and specific? Is Hero 2's "what they gain so they return" present and specific? Is Hero 3 (keywords/AEO) riding on top of real Hero 1+2 substance, not substituting for it? Order matters — if Hero 3 appears before 1 and 2 are served, FAIL.
2. **Gate check** (word-scale): does every element pass Gates 1, 2, 3, 4 individually?
3. **Devil's Advocate check:** does the page contain any "same but better / it integrates / bla bla" sentences? If yes, FAIL with the exact lines flagged.

That is how the obsession scales past what one human can hand-do.

## SECTION 4 — SCHEMA, AEO & TECHNICAL LOGIC (PER PAGE TYPE)

The stack is already correct (Next.js App Router, Vercel, AVIF, JSON-LD). DO NOT rebuild it. Add the brain to the content layer and the schema layer.

**Schema per page type (JSON-LD, every page):**
- **Hub / parent home:** `Organization` + `ProfessionalService` + `LocalBusiness` (Malta). Include `sameAs` (socials), real address/area served.
- **Pillar + product pages:** `Service` + `FAQPage` (every page should carry 2–4 real Q&A pairs in schema).
- **Pain/search pages:** `FAQPage` + `Article` where it reads as a diagnosis piece. The H1 = the literal query.
- **Location pages:** `LocalBusiness` with `areaServed` set to the specific locality.
- **Case studies:** `Article` + (when real) `Review` / `AggregateRating` — **only with genuine data.** Never fabricate reviews or ratings (EU consumer law + detection systems suppress fakes + Malta is too small to not get caught — fakes work *against* the goal).

**`/llms.txt` (root):** a clean, plain-text map of the site for AI crawlers — what OARC/H360 is, the core entities, the key pages, the one-line answers to the top questions. This is cheap and it's a real AEO edge because few competitors have it.

**`robots.txt`:** keep the existing config that allows GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot. (Already done — verify it survives the rebuild.)

**The FAQ page + per-page Q&A — the single biggest AEO lever:**
Every question must be phrased EXACTLY as an owner would type it into ChatGPT/Google, and the answer must be self-contained and liftable. Examples:
- "Why is my restaurant not showing on Google Maps in Malta?"
- "How do I get more Google reviews for my restaurant?"
- "How can a restaurant in Malta stop losing money to Wolt and Bolt?"
- "What's the best way to get more walk-ins to a restaurant in Malta?"
- "Who does restaurant marketing in Malta?"  ← we want to BE the answer to this one.

**Zero-click reality (why AEO matters more than classic SEO now):** AI Overviews and AI answers increasingly resolve the query without a click. Position #1 organic isn't the prize anymore — being the *cited source inside the AI answer* is. That's why Gate 2 is co-equal with Gate 1, and why topical authority (narrow + deep) is the whole game.

## SECTION 8 — WORKED EXAMPLE (the four-gate rule applied to ONE real page, so Cursor & OpenClaw see the standard)

**Page:** `/h360/why-is-my-restaurant-empty`

- **Primary query (Gate 4):** "why is my restaurant empty" / "why aren't people coming to my restaurant malta"
- **H1 (Gate 4 + Gate 1):** *"Why Is Your Restaurant Empty? Here's What's Actually Broken — and How We Fix It."*
- **First 100 words (Gate 1 + Gate 3):** name the pain in his words (empty tables on a Tuesday, the worse place across the road full, money bleeding to Wolt), then pivot: *most agencies sell you marketing; we diagnose why you're invisible first — because we run restaurants in Malta and we know exactly what's broken.*
- **Body = the diagnosis (Gate 3 + the moat):** the real reasons a Malta restaurant sits empty — not on Maps / no reviews / invisible to tourists / leaking to aggregators / no repeat system — each with the *fix*.
