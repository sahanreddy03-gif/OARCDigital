---
name: Work experience rollout
description: The agreed staged approach for rebuilding OARC's work experience.
---

Begin with the `/our-work` visual experience and one visible PJAZZA proof card. Do not expand into the remaining portfolio or redesign individual case-study pages until this first visual direction is reviewed.

**Why:** The user wants to judge the visual credibility, editorial pacing, media treatment, and interaction of the opening work page before committing to a broader portfolio rebuild.

**How to apply:** Treat a new work-card or case-detail redesign as a separate, explicitly approved phase. Keep the work index grounded in a calm, media-led, minimal editorial rhythm rather than reverting to generic agency gradients, overlays, chips, or card templates.

For the New Work collection, the approved opening direction is a single typographic editorial statement with generous negative space; the complete archive follows directly, without a hero collage or competing floating images.

**Why:** The user explicitly preferred the clean, high-end pacing of the supplied Instrument reference over a collage-led agency landing page.

**How to apply:** Keep the first viewport quiet and intentional, use one clear scroll cue, and let the work archive—not decorative hero imagery—be the next visual event.
