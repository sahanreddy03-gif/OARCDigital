## The rule

Treat each department card as a distinct, authored visual object. Let the image carry the art direction without textual overlays; place the department name and a concise commercial promise beneath it as the clear navigation cue.

**Why:** The user wants premium, reference-level visual impact without making the cards feel like generic website thumbnails. Putting the actionable title below the image makes it obvious that the department can be opened while preserving the image's cinematic space.

**How to apply:** Keep department imagery tall, unique, and commercially meaningful. Use a restrained text footer with the department title as the primary interactive signal; do not reintroduce counters, small metadata, or copy layered over the artwork unless a future direction explicitly changes this rule.
