---
name: Tom industry content fidelity
description: Rules for separating exact customer copy from internal research and visual-production instructions on the ten industry pages.
---

The ten industry pages must preserve the supplied v2 customer copy and its industry-specific differences. Domain briefs inform the build but are not customer-facing sections. Named visual briefs are production instructions and must never be printed as explanatory copy on the live page.

**Why:** A shared renderer previously exposed internal research and visual directions while adding generic filler that was not in the brief. This made distinct pages look templated and obscured the authored content.

**How to apply:** Complete and validate all nine content blocks for all ten industries before beginning the visual rebuild. Keep Tom in the exact locations specified by the current brief; revisit broader product naming only as a separate, explicit content decision.
