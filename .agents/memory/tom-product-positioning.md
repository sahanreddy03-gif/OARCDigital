name: Tom product positioning
description: Tom is an internal shorthand, while the customer-facing product is OARC Operator System
---

Treat Tom as a demo/personality label, not the product itself. The product promise is a managed operating layer that receives work, understands business rules, takes permitted actions in existing systems, proves the result, and keeps people in control.

**Why:** A named AI character makes the offer sound like marketing. Buyers need to understand the mechanism, the evidence, the permissions, and the completed business outcome.

**How to apply:** Lead `/tom` and future operator material with OARC Operator System language. Use Tom only when a human shorthand helps, and always connect the claim back to a real workflow, system action, audit trail, or handoff.
