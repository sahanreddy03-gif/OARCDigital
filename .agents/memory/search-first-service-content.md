---
name: Search-first service content
description: Durable rules for naming and writing OARC service pages around discoverability, buyer psychology, and defensible evidence.
---

Name and structure service pages from the language buyers use before they know OARC: the problem, desired outcome, category term, industry, and location. Brand concepts such as Tom or OARC Operator System may support the explanation but must not replace searchable service language.

**Why:** Narrative product terminology can sound distinctive while failing to match what buyers type or what AI answers retrieve. Unsupported claims, borrowed metrics, and invented case studies weaken trust rather than creating authority.

**How to apply:** Build a keyword and intent map from live search results, competitor language, first-party search data when available, and buyer questions before writing. Use plain category-led titles and H1s, answer the decision questions directly, and label every proof item as verified OARC evidence, a sourced external fact, or a simulation.
